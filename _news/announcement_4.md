My research visiting period in **Princeton University** took place in the group of Professor [H. Vincent Poor](https://ece.princeton.edu/people/h-vincent-poor). The focus of my stay was to work on neural methods for channel capacity estimation using $f$-divergence. Estimating channel capacity with $f$-divergence is particularly complex as there is no general guarantee that maximizing the $f$-MI leads to the capacity-achieving input distribution. We provide and analyze 3 different classes of objective functions, and design a set of self-consistency tests to verify the internal consistency of neural capacity estimators. 

I am extremely grateful for the opportunity, for the constructive discussions and for the help of Professor Poor and Alex Dytso.
